🚀 Onboarding & Acquisition Drip

    Registration confirmation & 100% welcome package overview.
  • 2. Registered No Deposit — 1st Reminder
    Initial follow-up pushing the deposit match incentive.
  • 3. Registered No Deposit — 2nd Reminder
    Assisted onboarding featuring crypto & Web3 wallet guides.
  • 4. Registered No Deposit — 3rd Reminder
    Urgency booster highlighting big wins and reserved bonus status.
  • 5. Registered No Deposit — Final Reminder
    Broad ecosystem showcase covering prediction markets and cashback.
⚡ High-Intent Conversion Triggers
  • 6. Bonus Claimed — Pending Deposit (1h Delay)
    High-intent recovery template featuring 24/7 support assistance.
  • 7. Bonus Claimed — Deposit Completed
    Confirmation notice celebrating successful funding and active status.
🎯 Post-FTD Retention Flow
  • 8. Post-FTD Day 1 Engagement
    Spotlights WhalePlay Originals, live dealer tables, and popular slots.
  • 9. Post-FTD Day 3 Engagement
    Drives long-term habits via Weekly Cashback safety nets and Leaderboards.
  • 10. Second Deposit Nudge
    Targets the "FTD, Played, No 2nd Deposit" segment — players who used their first deposit but haven't reloaded. Leads with SABA Prediction Markets, Cashback, Leaderboards and ACCA Boost.
  • 11. Post-2nd Deposit Milestone
    Sent 24 hours post-2nd deposit focusing on ecosystem highlights and engagement.
  • 12. Post-3rd Deposit Milestone
    Sent 3 days post-3rd deposit highlighting Sportsbook ACCA Boosts and multi-vertical play.
  • 13. Post-4th Deposit & Loyalty Milestone
    Sent 5 days post-4th deposit focusing on Weekly Cashback and League Leaderboards.
⚠️ Early Warning — Churn Risk
  • 14. Declining Activity Check-In
    Targets the "Declining" segment — login sessions trending down. Soft, no-pressure tone; earlier and lighter touch than the High Risk template.
  • 15. High Risk (Churn Threat) Check-In
    Targets previously-frequent depositors who've gone quiet the last few days. Fires before the D+7/14/30 drip — leads with League Rank + Cashback, backed by Originals, Playson, and ACCA Boost.
📬 Ongoing Retention & Reactivation Bucket
  • 16. Weekly Engagement Newsletter
    Modular broadcast template — this week's big match (2 Goals Up + ACCA Boost) and WhalePlay Originals spotlight. Update the match block weekly.
  • 17. Reactivation Drip — D+7
    Soft, low-pressure check-in reminding lapsed players what's still live: Cashback, Originals, Playson, ACCA Boost.
  • 18. Reactivation Drip — D+14
    Medium push centered on the no-deposit League Reward Bonus, plus Cashback, Playson, and Referral reminders.
  • 19. Reactivation Drip — D+30
    Final call built around the real weekly Cashback reset, plus League Reward, 2 Goals Up, Playson, and ACCA Boost.
📣 Ad Hoc Broadcasts
  • 20. Boosted Odds
    Sent whenever the trading team confirms a fresh batch of boosted-odds matches — not on a fixed schedule. Leads with a celebration banner (reuses the 2 Goals Up photo, cropped), then swap the match cards (team names, market, boosted odds, kickoff) each send; CTA currently points to /en/promotions until a dedicated Boosted Odds page goes live.
