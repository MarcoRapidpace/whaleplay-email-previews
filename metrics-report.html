What to discuss

One player drives the result

A single SABA sports player (Bosnia) staked 4,150 of the 5,725 real-money turnover (72.5%) and won 6,610 back, a net 2,460 gain for the player. That is more than the whole platform loss of 2,095. Excluding that player, the platform is up about 365 USDTE on real money.

More cash out than in

Withdrawals (4,148.63) exceed deposits (2,468.68) by 1,679.95. One withdrawal of 3,755.85 (90.5% of the total) was requested on 30 Sep by a player with no October bets.

Slow withdrawals

Three of four withdrawals took 6.4 to 7.9 days from request to completion. The fourth took 7.6 hours. Worth confirming whether this is manual review or a queue problem.

Registrations keep falling

Registrations were 307 in July, 69 in August, 27 in September and 19 so far in October (to 22 Oct). Affiliate-tagged signups went 203, 18, 9, 3 over the same months. Of the 19 October signups, 3 deposited.

Affiliate traffic converts poorly

632 players (52.5% of the 1,203 base) are affiliate-tagged, but only 11 of them ever deposited (1.7%), versus 44 of 571 non-affiliated players (7.7%).

Bonuses rarely complete

Across the 8 bonuses, 219 claims have produced 2 completions (0.9%). Active bonus 9 reached 38 of 1,346 eligible players and 37 of those failed. Bonus 6 has 223 eligible and 0 claims.

Very thin activity

Only 5 players bet in 22 days, and the export shows no bets after 19 Oct (23:25 UTC). Please confirm Oct 20 to 22 is genuinely quiet and not a data gap.

High-frequency casino play

One Italian player placed 2,561 Blue Ocean casino bets between 15 and 19 Oct (up to 971 in one day) at around 0.1 to 0.3 USDTE per real bet. Small money (net loss 71, cashback 3.55), but it looks automated or bonus-driven and is worth a quick look.
